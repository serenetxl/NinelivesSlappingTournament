The Stories That Defined the Championship

👑
The Million-Coin Final
The biggest match in CatCoins history. 31 bets, 1,210,449 CC on the table — 638,131 on Gaia, 572,318 on Mici. Almost a coin flip. Then Gaia won 4–0. Nishi collected +185,200, Briquette +100,000. On the other side: YangKai −200,000, Chion −120,000, Serene −70,000. A best-of-seven that didn't need more than four.
💥
The Upset of the Tournament
QF-4: Jia Ying vs Mici. Only 12% of the pot was on Mici. Mici won 3–2 at 8.56×. YangKai turned 12,000 into +90,720. Ogeypeko turned 10,000 into +75,600. RooZaki lost 64,000, Numbs 57,322 — and Jia Ying, who backed themselves with 20,000, lost that too.
🔁
The Champion Gets Slapped
Before the finals came the Special Round — the matchup the guild voted to see: Feiilong vs Gaia, a rematch of Round 1, which Gaia won 2–0. This time Feiilong won 3–1 at the 10× cap. Only six bets were right. YangKai's 10,000 became +90,000. Ogeypeko lost 81,000, Player20 80,000, Serene 50,000. Gaia's answer? Win the final 4–0.
🔟
Briquette: Bet on Yourself
Round 1, Jahbs vs Briquette. Only 9% of the pot was on Briquette. Jahbs put 14,860 on themselves. Briquette put 1,000 on themselves — and won 2–1 at 10×. Five winning bets, including Serene and Gaia. Briquette went on to finish the event with the second-highest net worth in the guild: 201,936 CC.
🎢
YangKai: The Rollercoaster
Most bets of anyone (35). Most CC staked (431,387). Two of the four biggest wins of the event — and the single biggest loss. Lost 95,000 in the semis, then won it all back and more — 90,000 in the Special Round and 53,300 on Hydego for bronze — to peak at +164,385. Then 200,000 on Mici in the final. Final net: −35,615 CC. Exhausting just to read.
🪙
Zia-heart: Down to the Last 2 CC
Backed themselves three times in Round 1 — 2,000, then 13,000, then 5,652 — and lost to Mici 2–0. Later bet 1 CC on Mici in the semis and won exactly 1 CC. Finished the event with a balance of 2 CC. Every coin counts.
